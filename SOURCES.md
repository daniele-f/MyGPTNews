# Approved sources

Future daily tasks may collect only from these editable source groups. Official primary sources have the highest trust.

1. **Official game/publisher source:** official game and publisher websites, official Steam news and store pages.
2. **Major gaming event:** official event websites.
3. **Trusted gaming publication:** PC Gamer, Rock Paper Shotgun, Eurogamer, IGN, GameSpot, Polygon, Video Games Chronicle.
4. **Industry publication:** GamesIndustry.biz.
